Tumbleweed from nowhere

YenTing's original songs about life and projection' (2026)

Tumbleweed is a nomadic plant that wanders with the wind, abandoning its roots and relentlessly seeking the most suitable environment for itself. This tenacious spirit of survival and its wandering path allowed YenTing Lo to see a microcosm of life: seemingly unable to leave no trace, yet always quietly extending one’s presence in the world in some form.

風滾草(Tumbleweed)是一種隨著風流浪的植物,以遊牧的精神,捨棄根系、鍥而不捨地尋 覓最適合自己的環境。這強韌的求生精神及漂泊的路徑讓羅妍婷看見人生的縮影:看似無法留 下足跡,卻總以某種形式悄悄地將自己延續在世上。

The fourth album of the jazz singer-songwriter YenTing Lo, “Tumbleweed from nowhere”, is a meditation of life and existence, and the discrepancy between image and reality. The record oscillates between high-intensity emotion and quiet simplicity, repeatedly circling back to a passionate outlook on being. With YenTing’s unique narrative style, this album encompasses various musical styles including Jazz, Folk, Neo-Soul, and R&B, pursuing a colorful and modern sound while also exploring the literary aspects of its lyrics and music.


《Tumbleweed from nowhere》是爵士創作歌手羅妍婷的第四張專輯,主題環繞在生命的美醜,對存在的質疑,以及形象和真實的誤差。情緒時而濃烈高亢、時而泰然樸素。在不斷重組提問方式的過程中,卻又總是回到充滿熱忱的人生觀。這張專輯包含 Jazz、Folk、Neo-Soul 及 R&B 等多種曲風,追求充滿色彩的現代聲響,同時琢磨於詞曲創作的文學性。既具有親近於大眾的律動及共鳴,也延續著妍婷獨有的敘事境界。適合輕鬆感受音樂演奏的生命力,也適合細細品味詞曲中的縝密結構。
